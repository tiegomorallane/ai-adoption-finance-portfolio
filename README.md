## How I approach AI implementation

1. **Diagnose the workflow.** Understand users, bottlenecks, inputs, decisions, exceptions and ownership.
2. **Choose the smallest useful intervention.** Automate a meaningful part of the process, not merely a prompt.
3. **Build with appropriate controls.** Integrations, private data, grounded outputs, clear permissions and human review.
4. **Test real user journeys.** Verify errors, saved state, evidence quality and recovery as well as happy paths.
5. **Measure operational value.** Time, accuracy, adoption, recovery and decision usefulness.
