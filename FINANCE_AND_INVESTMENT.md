## Commercial finance in practice

As founder of **Mora Capital Partners (2026–present)**, my work includes client sourcing, financing-readiness assessments, lender-facing documentation, structured proposals and engagement with independent finance providers.

The process connects three questions:
1. **The business:** What does the company need funding for, and what commercial context matters?
2. **The evidence:** What supporting information is available, what needs clarification, and how can it be organised for review?
3. **The decision:** What do stakeholders need to move the process forward, and who owns the financing decision?

Approximately **R20 million in third-party lending disbursements facilitated** and **R1.2 million in company revenue** in the first six months are self-reported business outcomes. Facilitated finance is not AUM, company revenue or a private-equity investment return. Independent financing providers make lending decisions and disburse funds.
