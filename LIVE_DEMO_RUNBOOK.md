2. Explain only the approved product benefits; offer the starter pack when appropriate.
3. Agree a specific follow-up with consent, without pressure or invented guarantees.

Adapt to the customer response; do not read fixed dialogue irrespective of what the AI says. The observed rehearsal ended automatically after a next step was agreed. AI feedback is practice guidance; human assessors sign off competence.
